# Whisperlane v0.8.6

Whisperlane v0.8.6 adds a second local speech engine and, with it, the first
multilingual model: **Qwen3-ASR 0.6B**. Whisper models, the default, and every
existing flow stay exactly as they were — the new model is simply one more
choice in Settings.

## Meet Qwen3-ASR 0.6B

A compact multilingual model that runs entirely on your machine:

- **31 languages**, including Turkish, English, Chinese (Mandarin and
  Cantonese), Japanese, Korean, Arabic, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese,
  Russian, Hindi, Vietnamese, Thai and more.
- **811 MB**, downloaded only when you ask for it, like every other model.
- **Vulkan on the GPU**, with a processor fallback for machines without a
  usable graphics device.
- **Local and offline.** No account, no cloud, no audio leaves the machine.

Pick it in **Settings → Speech model** whenever a language matters more than
raw English accuracy, and switch back to Large v3 Turbo at any time.

## Honest notes

- Qwen3-ASR shines on Chinese and Cantonese. On Turkish, our own side-by-side
  takes still favored the default Whisper model, so the option is a choice,
  not a replacement.
- The first take after switching to the new engine pays a one-time warm-up
  (about four seconds on the development machine). Later takes are fast:
  roughly 0.1–0.2 seconds for a 4–11 second clip.
- Both engines can invent a closing phrase on audio that is only silence or
  noise. A take that never rises above the capture's silence floor is still
  rejected before either engine is asked.
- Model weights are Apache-2.0 and are never bundled with the installer. Their
  licence and conversion notice live in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

## Under the hood

- The installer now carries two engine pairs. Each engine family lives in its
  own versioned runtime directory under `%APPDATA%`, with its own pointer and
  its own cleanup, so upgrading one never disturbs the other.
- A downloaded model is checked against the SHA-256 its catalog pins before it
  is accepted.
- The engine log records which engine family and runtime pin served a take, so
  support can see exactly what ran.
- Your existing Whisper model, settings, and shortcuts are untouched.

## Packaging and verification

- Installers: NSIS setup and MSI for Windows 10/11 x64.
- Verify the download against `SHA256SUMS.txt`:

  ```powershell
  Get-FileHash Whisperlane_0.8.6_x64-setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256
  ```

- The installer itself is not Authenticode-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may
  ask for confirmation. The updater artifact is signed and verified by the app.
